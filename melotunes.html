Use a sharp, straight photo with one or more complete staves visible. Crop away large empty margins and avoid shadows.

3. Set the notation

Recognition process
1. Detect the five staff lines.
2. Find where the clef / key / time signature ends.
3. Detect barlines (measure lines; the line closing a row is ignored).
4. Identify individual noteheads and stems.
5. Recover missed notes (stems and note spacing).
6. Check each measure's rhythm against the time signature.
7. Calculate musical pitches.

4. Detect and play
