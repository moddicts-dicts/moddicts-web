How Melo reads your music
  1. Detects the five staff lines.
  2. Finds where the clef, key and time signature end.
  3. Spots barlines (the line closing a row is ignored).
  4. Identifies noteheads and stems.
  5. Recovers missed notes from stems and spacing.
  6. Checks each measure against the time signature.
  7. Calculates the musical pitches.
